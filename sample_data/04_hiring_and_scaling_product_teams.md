## The Three PM Archetypes (Shreyas Doshi)
When hiring product managers, companies frequently evaluate candidates against a generic rubric. In reality, great PMs usually excel along one of three distinct axes:
1. **The Product Sense / Innovation PM**: Exceptional at empathy, user taste, 0-to-1 product discovery, and turning vague ambiguities into intuitive experiences.
2. **The Growth / Metrics PM**: Deeply analytical, masters of conversion funnels, hypothesis testing, experiment velocity, and quantitative attribution.
3. **The Execution / Systems PM**: Incredible at complex technical dependencies, cross-functional alignment, high-reliability delivery, and large platform migrations.

Matching the archetype to the current lifecycle stage of the company is vital: hiring a pure Growth PM for an ambiguous 0-to-1 feature causes frustration, while hiring an unstructured Innovation PM for a mature enterprise checkout flow leads to execution delays.

### The Stripe Operating Principle: "Say the Thing"
Claire Hughes Johnson outlines that scaling companies fail not from external competition, but from creeping bureaucracy and unaddressed friction. Key operating tenets include:
- **Write things down**: High-leverage teams operate with clear decision documents (PRDs, 6-pagers) rather than endless consensus meetings.
- **Operating Cadence**: Align quarterly reviews around outcomes achieved, not roadmap features checked off.
- **Empowered Product Trios**: Autonomous squads comprising a Product Manager, Engineering Lead, and Product Designer with dedicated problem spaces and clear boundary lines.
